Admissions

Joining is easy

Start with a quick enquiry and we guide you through every step. Reception is happy to help at any point.

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Call reception or fill the admission enquiry form.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Submit the application for the board and medium you choose.

  3. 3

    Visit

    Meet the team, plus an entrance test or interview if your class needs one.

  4. 4

    Confirm

    Complete the documents and fee payment to secure the seat.

Documents to prepare

Commonly requested. Please confirm the full list with the school office for your class.

  • Birth certificate
  • Student Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Previous class marksheet or report card
  • Blood group certificate
  • Character certificate
